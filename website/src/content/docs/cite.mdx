LiteInjector was presented at the **28th Euromicro Conference on Digital System Design (DSD 2025)**. If you use it in your work, please cite:

> Adam Henault, Philippe Tanguy, Vianney Lapôtre. *LiteInjector: A LiteX Extension for Fault Injection*. 28th Euromicro Conference on Digital System Design (DSD), 2025.

```bibtex
@inproceedings{henault2025liteinjector,
  author    = {Henault, Adam and Tanguy, Philippe and Lap{\^o}tre, Vianney},
  title     = {{LiteInjector}: A {LiteX} Extension for Fault Injection},
  booktitle = {28th Euromicro Conference on Digital System Design (DSD)},
  year      = {2025}
}
```

The repository also provides a [`CITATION.cff`](https://github.com/labsticc-arcad/LiteInjector/blob/main/CITATION.cff) file, used by the "Cite this repository" button on GitHub.

## About

LiteInjector is developed by the ARCAD team of the Lab-STICC laboratory in Lorient, France, and released under the BSD-2-Clause license. It builds on [LiteX](https://github.com/enjoy-digital/litex) and [LiteScope](https://github.com/enjoy-digital/litescope) by Enjoy-Digital.
